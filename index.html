Eid Mubarak

عِيدٌ مُبَارَكٌ

May the blessings of Almighty Allah be upon you and your family


MD. MAHABUBUR RAHAMAN SIAM

Assalamu Alaikum Wa Rahmatullahi Wa Barakatuh 🌙

As the blessed crescent of Shawwal rises and the sacred month of Ramadan draws to its beautiful close, I reach out with a heart full of gratitude and joy, wishing you and your entire family a truly blessed Eid ul-Fitr.

May the sacrifices you made during this holy month be accepted, your prayers answered, and your heart filled with the peace that only true faith can bring. May Allah's mercy rain upon you like the gentle dew of a new morning.

تَقَبَّلَ اللَّهُ مِنَّا وَمِنكُم "May Allah accept (good deeds) from us and from you."

On this joyful day, as we gather with family and break bread together, I pray that every test you face in life becomes a stepping stone, every challenge a lesson in resilience, and every dream you carry, a reality worth celebrating.

Peace & Barakah Health & Happiness Success & Growth Allah's Rahma
وَلِتُكْمِلُوا الْعِدَّةَ وَلِتُكَبِّرُوا اللَّهَ عَلَىٰ مَا هَدَاكُمْ وَلَعَلَّكُمْ تَشْكُرُونَ

"…so that you may complete the prescribed period and glorify Allah for having guided you, and so that you may be grateful."

SURAH AL-BAQARAH  ·  2:185

تَقَبَّلَ اللَّهُ مِنَّا وَمِنكُم وَأَحَالَهُ اللَّهُ عَلَيْكُم

May Allah accept from all of us and bless you abundantly
